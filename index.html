The Vision

Hello, I'm Vinx — a photographer based in Budapest, Hungary, with a passion for telling stories through light, emotion, and detail.

My journey began long before I ever picked up a camera. Growing up surrounded by cars, I developed an appreciation for their design, character, and the emotions they inspire. What started as a lifelong love for the automotive world naturally evolved into a passion for photography, allowing me to preserve not only extraordinary machines but meaningful moments and genuine human connections as well.

Every project is approached with creativity, precision, and a strong eye for composition. I believe the best photographs are more than beautiful images — they're moments you can feel long after they've been captured.

Whether I'm photographing an automotive masterpiece, a portrait, a wedding, or a special event, my goal remains the same: to create timeless imagery that reflects the character, atmosphere, and story behind every frame.

Photography isn't just about pressing the button. It's about noticing the details others might overlook and transforming them into images worth remembering.
