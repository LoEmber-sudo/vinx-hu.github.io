Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

vinx.hu ("we", "us") operates https://vinx.hu. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: such as your name, email address, and message when you use our contact form or sign up.
  • Booking and order details: such as your name, contact details, and session or purchase information when you book a session or buy from our shop.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through analytics.
  • Cookies: small files stored on your device. See "Cookies" below.

How we use it

  • To respond to your messages and provide our services.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam.

Third-party services

Our site may use services such as a booking or scheduling tool, an online shop and payment provider, Instagram, and web analytics. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site (for example hosting, analytics, and email), or when required by law.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work and, with your consent, analytics cookies. You can decline non-essential cookies in our banner or in your browser settings.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data to perform a contract with you (bookings, orders), with your consent (analytics cookies, newsletters), and for our legitimate interests (site security, replying to enquiries).

Your rights

As a user in the EU you (under GDPR) may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain uses. Email us at vinx.hu.contact@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, naih.hu).

Data retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above and use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13 (16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
